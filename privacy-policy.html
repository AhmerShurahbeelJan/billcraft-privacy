BillCraft Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

BillCraft is a bill and invoice maker for Android. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes. In short: everything you enter stays on your phone, and we do not collect, sell, or share your data.

Information you enter

BillCraft stores the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Bills: customer names, items, quantities, prices, discounts, taxes, totals and dates.
  • Your shop profile, if you fill it in: shop name, owner name, phone, email, address, tax number and a shop photo.

This information is never sent to us or to any server we run. BillCraft does not require an account and does not have one.

Shop photo

If you add a shop photo, you choose it from your gallery or take it with your camera. BillCraft keeps a resized copy in its private storage to show on your profile and print on your invoices. It asks for no photo or camera permission; your phone's own picker and camera app hand over only the photo you choose.

Invoice PDFs

Each bill you save is also saved as a PDF invoice in your phone's Download/BillCraft folder, so you can open, print or send it. Unlike the app's private storage, this folder can be seen by you and by file manager apps on your phone. Invoices leave your phone only when you share them yourself, for example through WhatsApp or email. On Android 9 and older, BillCraft asks for storage permission to save there; newer versions need no permission.

Microphone and voice entry

BillCraft asks for microphone access only when you tap the mic to speak a bill. If you decline, you can still type bills by hand.

Voice entry uses your phone's built-in speech recognition service (for example, Google's speech services). Depending on your phone and its settings, that service may process your voice on the device or send it online to recognize your words. That processing is handled by the service's provider under their own privacy policy. BillCraft receives only the resulting text, uses it to fill in the bill, and does not record or store audio.

Data we collect

None. BillCraft contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reporting tools, and we do not receive any information about you or how you use the app.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone.

Backups

If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include BillCraft's data in your device backup to your Google account. This is controlled by your phone's settings and Google's backup service, not by BillCraft.

Keeping or deleting your data

Your bills and profile stay on your phone until you remove them. Uninstalling BillCraft, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all of its data from the device. Invoice PDFs in Download/BillCraft are your files: they stay after uninstalling, and you can delete them with any file manager.

Children

BillCraft is a business tool intended for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If BillCraft starts handling data differently, for example by adding cloud backup or sharing, this policy will be updated before that change is released, and the date above will change.
